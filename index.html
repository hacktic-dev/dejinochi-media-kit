About

Dejinochi

Dejinochi is a retro-style virtual pet that lives on your desktop. Feed it, play with it, clean up after it and look after it when it gets sick. The way you care for your pet influences what it eventually grows into. When a pet reaches adulthood it can graduate and move on, but the pets you’ve raised are remembered and may return to visit you later, sometimes bringing gifts with them.

Key features

What’s in the game

  • A virtual pet that lives alongside you on your Windows desktop.
  • Care-driven evolution: how you feed, treat and look after your pet influences what it grows into.
  • Pets eventually graduate, are remembered in your collection, and can return to visit.
  • Returning pets and normal play can reward you with gifts and unlockables.
  • Collect different shells and decorate them with collectible stickers.
  • Play small minigames with your pet.
  • Asynchronous multiplayer / pet visits through Steam.
  • Steam achievements.
  • Steam Cloud saves.
  • Steam Rich Presence integration.
  • Mod support.
